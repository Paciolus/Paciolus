# Sprint 893 — engine procedure wording, old and new

Sprint 887 (OUT-30) rewrote `backend/shared/follow_up_procedures.py` so every suggestion opens "The auditor may consider" and gives no command. The TB diagnostic, accrual completeness and population profile engines carried their own imperative procedures; this table covers all of them. The expense category engine emits no procedures.

Each rewrite keeps every fact, number, threshold and citation (ASC 205-40, 275-10, 606, 850; AS 2305). A text may open with a plain statement of fact before the suggestion. `{...}` marks a value the engine interpolates, and those values are unchanged. `test_engine_procedures_are_suggestions` in `backend/tests/test_memo_banned_phrases.py` enforces the rule on every text below.
